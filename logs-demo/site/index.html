Send logs from your app

Every button below calls dg.log. Lines go to your dashboard: My apps → Logs (only approved apps show the button). Without sign-in they are marked anon; after signing in, user.

Repeats and batches

Identical lines are merged into one row with a count. Lines are sent in batches (every 5 seconds, 20 at most, and when the page closes).

What this page asked dg.log to send (the dashboard shows what the server kept).

    Never log passwords or personal data: anyone can open your app, and anyone can try to send lines to its log. dg.run only accepts them from your app's own pages and limits how many arrive, but it can't prove a line came from your real code. Treat logs as hints.
