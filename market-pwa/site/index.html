Tally

Count anything. Your counts stay on your device, even offline.

Use it in the browser

Tally is installed on this device. Open it from your app list or home screen. Or use it here in the browser.

  • Its own windowIt opens like any other app, with its own icon, not in a browser tab.
  • Works offlineEverything it needs is saved on your device the first time you open it.
  • PrivateYour counters never leave this device. There is no account.

For developers: this page lives at / and asks for the install. The app is a separate page in /app; once installed, it always starts there, so installed users never see this page.
